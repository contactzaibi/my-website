02 / Research

What helps firms innovate?

My final-year thesis asks whether R&D spending and a firm’s ability to absorb outside knowledge make Pakistani firms more likely to innovate. I worked with World Bank Enterprise Survey data for Pakistan from 2015, covering 696 firms.

  • 696 firms
  • World Bank Enterprise Survey 2015
  • Probit regression
  • Average marginal effects
Research context

The Nexus of R&D, Absorptive Capacity, and Innovation: Evidence from Pakistani Enterprises. Supervised by Dr. Muhammad Zeeshan Younas.

A Probit model estimates the chance that a firm innovates. Average marginal effects then show how that chance changes when a factor such as R&D spending changes. This page describes the question and method only.
